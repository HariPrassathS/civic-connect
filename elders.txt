1. THE PROBLEM
================================================================================

Civic Connect currently requires users to:
  - Read text on screen
  - Type complaints
  - Navigate menus
  - Upload photos with descriptions
  - Understand UI elements like buttons, tabs, forms

This works great for educated, tech-savvy citizens. But India's real civic
issues are most often faced by:
  - Senior citizens (60+) who can't read small text or navigate apps
  - Non-literate or semi-literate citizens who can't type
  - Rural users unfamiliar with smartphone apps
  - Visually impaired users

These are the people who MOST need civic services but are LEAST able to
use digital platforms. We must bridge this gap.


2. THE SOLUTION — VOICE-FIRST ASSISTANT
================================================================================

When a citizen opens the app, a friendly AI voice assistant automatically
greets them in their preferred language (Tamil / English / Hindi). The
assistant takes over the ENTIRE complaint process through natural
conversation. The citizen simply TALKS and optionally takes a PHOTO.

No reading. No typing. No navigation. Just talk.


3. USER FLOW — ELDER MODE (DETAILED)
================================================================================

STEP 1: APP LAUNCH & DETECTION
─────────────────────────────────
  - Citizen opens the app (PWA or native)
  - A splash screen appears for 2 seconds with a large, friendly message:
      "🎤 Tap anywhere to talk to our assistant"
      (Also displayed in Tamil: "எங்கள் உதவியாளரிடம் பேச எங்கும் தட்டவும்")
  - If the user taps the screen → Voice Assistant activates (Elder Mode)
  - If the user taps "Skip" (small text at bottom) → Normal login page loads
  - Auto-timeout: If no interaction for 5 seconds, voice assistant
    auto-activates with a greeting audio

STEP 2: VOICE GREETING & LANGUAGE SELECTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Assistant speaks:
    "Vanakkam! Naan Civic Connect assistant. Unga complaint-a
     register panna naan help pannren. Tamil la pesalama,
     English la pesalama?"

    (Translation: "Hello! I am the Civic Connect assistant.
     I will help you register your complaint. Shall we speak
     in Tamil or English?")

  - Speech Recognition listens for: "Tamil" / "English" / "Hindi"
  - Sets language for the rest of the conversation
  - Falls back to Tamil if no response detected

STEP 3: COLLECT CITIZEN IDENTITY (CONVERSATIONAL)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Assistant speaks:
    "Unga paeru enna?"  (What is your name?)
  Citizen says:
    "Raman"

  Assistant speaks:
    "Raman, unga phone number sollunga"  (Tell me your phone number)
  Citizen says:
    "98765 43210"

  Assistant speaks:
    "Unga area enna? District enna?"  (What is your area? District?)
  Citizen says:
    "Anna Nagar, Chennai"

  → The assistant uses GPS as a fallback/verification:
    "Naan unga location detect panniten — Anna Nagar, Chennai.
     Correct aa?"  (I detected your location — is this correct?)

STEP 4: COLLECT COMPLAINT (CONVERSATIONAL)
───────────────────────────────────────────
  Assistant speaks:
    "Enna problem irukku? Sollunga, naan note pannikiren"
    (What is the problem? Tell me, I will note it down)

  Citizen says:
    "Enga veettu munnadi road la periya gundhu irukku,
     rendu naal aa yaarum fix pannala"
    (There is a big pothole in front of our house,
     nobody has fixed it for two days)

  → AI processes the speech and extracts:
    - Issue Type: Road / Pothole
    - Description: Large pothole in front of house, unfixed for 2 days
    - Location: Already captured from Step 3
    - Urgency: Medium (inferred from "two days")

  Assistant confirms:
    "Unga veettu munnadi road la pothole irukku nu puriyuthu.
     Correct aa?"
    (I understand there is a pothole in front of your house. Correct?)

  Citizen says: "Aama" (Yes)

STEP 5: PHOTO CAPTURE (OPTIONAL, GUIDED)
──────────────────────────────────────────
  Assistant speaks:
    "Problem-oda photo edukka mudiyuma? Phone-a problem pakka
     point pannunga, naan photo eduthukiren"
    (Can you take a photo of the problem? Point your phone
     at the problem, I will take the photo)

  → Camera opens automatically in a simplified full-screen view
  → Large "📸" button or voice command "photo edu" to capture
  → AI validates the photo (is it relevant? is it clear?)

  If photo is blurry:
    "Photo konjam clear illa, innum oru thadava try pannunga"
    (Photo is not clear, please try once more)

  If no photo:
    "Paravala, photo illama kuda complaint register pannalam"
    (No problem, we can register the complaint even without a photo)

STEP 6: CONFIRMATION & SUBMISSION
──────────────────────────────────
  Assistant speaks:
    "Raman, unga complaint ready:
     Road pothole — Anna Nagar, Chennai.
     Submit pannava?"
    (Your complaint is ready. Shall I submit?)

  Citizen says: "Aama, pannu" (Yes, do it)

  → Complaint is submitted to the backend
  → A complaint ID is generated

  Assistant speaks:
    "Unga complaint successfully register aachichu!
     Complaint number: CC-2026-4521.
     Naanga itha 48 hours la resolve panna try pannuven.
     Nandri!"
    (Your complaint has been successfully registered!
     We will try to resolve it within 48 hours. Thank you!)

STEP 7: FOLLOW-UP (FUTURE VISITS)
───────────────────────────────────
  When the citizen opens the app again:
  
  Assistant speaks:
    "Vanakkam Raman! Unga last complaint — road pothole —
     ippo 'In Progress' la irukku. Field worker Ravi Kumar
     assign aayirukkar. Vera enna help venum?"
    (Hello Raman! Your last complaint is now In Progress.
     Field worker Ravi Kumar has been assigned. What else
     can I help with?)


4. TECH STACK FOR VOICE ASSISTANT
================================================================================

  COMPONENT                  TECHNOLOGY OPTIONS
  ─────────────────────────  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Speech-to-Text (STT)       Web Speech API (free, browser-native)
                              OR Google Cloud Speech-to-Text (paid, better)
                              OR Whisper API (OpenAI, multilingual)

  Text-to-Speech (TTS)       Web Speech API (free, browser-native)
                              OR Google Cloud TTS (natural voices)
                              OR ElevenLabs (premium natural voices)

  AI Understanding (NLU)     Gemini API (already integrated in project)
                              → Extract intent, category, urgency, location
                              → Multilingual understanding (Tamil/English)

  Language Detection          Gemini API or simple keyword matching

  Photo Validation            Gemini Vision API (already have access)
                              → "Is this a photo of a civic issue?"
                              → "Is the photo clear enough?"

  Location                   Browser Geolocation API (already implemented)
                              + Nominatim reverse geocoding (already built)

  State Management            React Context or Zustand for conversation flow

6. UI DESIGN — ELDER MODE
================================================================================

  PRINCIPLE: Minimum visual, maximum audio.

  - Background: Calm, solid color (not dark mode — elders prefer light)
  - Center of screen: Large animated microphone icon (pulsing when listening)
  - Bottom: Large text showing what the assistant is saying (for semi-literate)
  - Font size: Minimum 24px for all text
  - Colors: High contrast (dark text on light background)
  - No small buttons, no hamburger menus, no tabs
  - Single tap anywhere = "I'm talking"
  - Swipe down = "Go back"
  - Shake phone = "Cancel and restart"

  VISUAL LAYOUT:
  ┌────────────────────────────────┐
  │                                │
  │    🎤  (Large pulsing mic)     │
  │                                │
  │   "Tell me your problem..."    │  ← Live subtitle of assistant
  │                                │
  │   ┌──────────────────────┐     │
  │   │  "Road la gundhu      │     │  ← Live transcript of citizen
  │   │   irukku..."          │     │
  │   └──────────────────────┘     │
  │                                │
  │   [ 📸 Take Photo ]            │  ← Only shown when needed
  │                                │
  └────────────────────────────────┘


7. ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES
================================================================================

  FEATURE                     DESCRIPTION
  ─────────────────────────   ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Auto-Voice Activation       No need to find and press buttons
  Large Touch Targets          Entire screen is a tap target
  High Contrast Mode           Light background, dark text, large fonts
  Haptic Feedback              Phone vibrates when assistant starts/stops
  Repeat Last Message          Citizen says "Innum oru thadava sollu"
  Speed Control                Slower speech rate for elderly users
  Offline Fallback             If no internet, record audio locally and
                               sync when connection is restored
  Emergency Shortcut           Triple-tap for emergency complaints
  Family Sharing               Send complaint copy to family member via SMS


8. DATABASE CHANGES NEEDED
================================================================================

  NEW TABLE: voice_complaints
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Column              Type              Description
  ─────────────────── ────────────────  ───────────────────────────────
  id                  uuid (PK)         Auto-generated
  citizen_name        text              Spoken name
  citizen_phone       text              Spoken phone number
  language            text              Selected language (ta/en/hi)
  audio_url           text              Stored audio recording URL
  transcript          text              Full conversation transcript
  ai_extracted_type   text              AI-detected issue category
  ai_extracted_desc   text              AI-generated description
  photo_url           text              Uploaded photo URL
  lat                 double precision  GPS latitude
  lng                 double precision  GPS longitude
  address             text              Reverse-geocoded address
  status              text              Complaint status
  complaint_id        uuid (FK)         Links to main complaints table
  created_at          timestamptz       Auto-generated

  → Voice complaints are eventually converted into regular complaints
    in the main `complaints` table so officials see them normally.


9. GEMINI AI PROMPT TEMPLATE (FOR NLU)
================================================================================

  System Prompt:
  "You are a civic complaint extraction AI. A citizen has described their
   problem in spoken Tamil/English. Extract the following fields from their
   speech:
   
   1. issue_type: One of [road, water, electricity, garbage, drainage,
      sanitation, streetlight, other]
   2. description: A clear English summary of the problem (2-3 sentences)
   3. urgency: One of [low, medium, high, urgent]
   4. landmarks: Any landmarks or specific locations mentioned
   
   Respond in JSON format only."

  User Input:
  "{citizen_speech_transcript}"
